Both assets move by the same percentage simultaneously. This is the correlated move scenario — when both move together by the same %, your token counts do not change at all and there is zero IL. The more correlated the moves, the less IL you experience.
Full matrix: rows = pHEX price multiples, columns = PLS price multiples. Each cell shows IL%. Green = low IL, amber = moderate, red = high IL. Notice the diagonal (where both move equally) is always 0%.
The key insight this tool reveals: Impermanent loss is entirely determined by the relative price change between your two assets, not their individual USD moves. If pHEX goes 5× and PLS goes 5×, your token counts are identical to entry and IL is exactly 0%. If pHEX goes 10× and PLS goes 5×, the ratio r = 2 and IL is only 5.7% — far less than if only pHEX moved 10× (IL would be 13.4%). The more correlated your two assets, the better your LP position performs relative to holding.
