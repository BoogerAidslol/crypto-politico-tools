Thin liquidity reality

Can produce double-digit % moves on major pairs. Same amount reverses gains just as fast when sellers appear.
Price heaviness effect
3–5× harder
After another 3–5× from here, the same absolute capital produces smaller % moves. Sustaining requires exponentially more inflow.
DEX concentration
~82% PulseX

Fragility signal

Further upside condition
