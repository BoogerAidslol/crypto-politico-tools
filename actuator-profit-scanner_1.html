Impact ($) — how much the price would move if you bought "impact test size" worth right now, computed from the actual pool reserves (constant-product AMM math). Big number = thin market, real risk on exit.
Fair Gap — market discount minus a "textbook" discount at your reference HEX APY. Positive = market's discount is bigger than a simple time-value calc would justify (looks cheap, but ask why — thin liquidity or real risk is the likely answer, not free money). Near zero everywhere = you'd expect in an efficient market; big scattered gaps = evidence it isn't one.

These aren't independent bets. ACTR, HDRN, and ICSA all only have value if people keep actively staking HEX through their respective wrappers — none of them create demand from outside the HEX ecosystem. A HEX price decline hits every one of these the same way (denominator risk), and separately, if HEX staking activity itself dries up, all three lose their reason to exist together, regardless of price. Comparing their liquidity side by side doesn't diversify anything — it shows the same underlying bet, wearing three outfits.

Liquidity/FDV — how much of the fully diluted value is actually backed by real, sellable liquidity right now. Lower % = more of the "market cap" is theoretical, since most holders couldn't actually sell at anywhere near that valuation. Est. Impact is a rough approximation (assumes roughly balanced pool reserves at the current USD liquidity figure) — for exact numbers on a specific pair, check the Market Scanner's Impact column, which uses real on-chain reserves.
