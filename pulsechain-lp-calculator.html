How the math works: This calculator uses the Uniswap V2 constant-product formula (x × y = k) which is the same AMM model PulseX uses. When pHEX price changes by a ratio r (new/old), your pHEX amount becomes original × (1/√r) and your PLS becomes original × √r. Impermanent loss % = (2√r/(1+r)) − 1. All calculations assume a 50/50 LP position by value at entry, which is how all standard AMM pools work. Fees earned are not compounded in these calculations. IL only crystallises when you withdraw — while you remain in the LP these are unrealised movements, not actual losses.
